# Privacy Policy for PetTrack

**Last Updated:** September 10, 2026

Bence Szabó ("we", "us", or "our") built the PetTrack app as an Open Source app. This SERVICE is provided by Bence Szabó at no cost and is intended for use as is.

This page is used to inform visitors regarding our policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of Personal Information if anyone decided to use our Service.

## 1. How PetTrack Works (Self-Hosted & Local)
PetTrack is designed primarily as a **self-hosted** pet monitoring solution. This means that, by default, the app communicates directly with a server hosted by **you** (the user). 
- **Video/Audio Streams & Images:** Media captured by the monitor device is sent directly to your configured server. 
- **Pet Data:** Information such as pet names, types, vaccines, medications, and activity logs are stored on your configured server (via SQLite database).
- **We do NOT collect, transmit, or store your personal data, video feeds, or pet information on our own servers** when you self-host the backend. All your data belongs to you and stays on your devices.

## 2. Permissions We Request
To provide its core functionality, the PetTrack app requires certain device permissions:
- **Camera & Microphone:** Required on the "Monitor" device to stream live video and audio of your pet, and to record movement events.
- **Storage/Photos:** Required to save pictures, replays, or profile avatars locally.
- **Network/Internet:** Required to communicate between the Client app, the Monitor app, and the Server.
- **Wear OS / Bluetooth:** Required if you use the smartwatch companion app to sync data between your phone and your watch.

## 4. Security
We value your trust in providing us your information (if any). Since PetTrack is an open-source, self-hosted platform, the security of your data relies heavily on how you configure and secure your own server (e.g., using strong PINs, VPNs like Tailscale, or HTTPS). We strive to use commercially acceptable means of protecting data during transmission, but remember that no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.
